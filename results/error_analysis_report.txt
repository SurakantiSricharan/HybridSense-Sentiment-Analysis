2. QUALITATIVE ERROR DIAGNOSIS & ROOT CAUSES
------------------------------------------------------------
A. Ambivalent -> Neutral / Positive / Negative:
   - Clause Asymmetry: Tweets where one clause has mild sentiment while the contrasting
     clause contains strong emotional words often bias the sequential LSTM toward the dominant clause.
   - Implicit Contrast: Sarcasm or subtweets that express mixed evaluation without explicit
     markers (e.g. 'Oh great, another delay just when my flight was about to leave') are frequently
     absorbed into purely Negative or Neutral.
   - Short Text Length: Tweets under 10 words lack sufficient token context for the Attention mechanism
     to balance multiple sentiment poles.

B. Negative / Positive -> Ambivalent (False Positives):
   - Rhetorical Conjunctions: Tweets using 'but' or 'though' in non-contrastive ways (e.g. 'I love you but
     seriously, happy birthday!') trigger high attention weights on both the greeting and the phrase,
     leading to an ambivalent prediction.
   - Slang & Complex Negations: Idiomatic expressions containing negative words (e.g. 'this is sick',
     'not bad at all') confuse n-gram feature extractors when paired with emojis.
